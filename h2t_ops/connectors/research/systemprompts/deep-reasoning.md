Full multi-step reasoning: decompose the question, pursue each sub-question across 3+ independent sources, and cite a URL for every factual assertion. Reason explicitly about source reliability and recency. When sources disagree, present each position with its citations and explain the nature of the disagreement — do not pick a winner without evidence. Structure output as: (1) consensus points with shared sources, (2) contested points with per-side sources and why they conflict, (3) open questions with URLs to pursue.
