## TL;DR
Reusable reference knowledge (domain theory, validated algorithms, established patterns) must be routed to a council-verified knowledge base with a citation, not left in siloed per-project research docs. Promotion requires a gated ingest (not a raw dump). Per-project docs may reference KB entries but must not duplicate them.
